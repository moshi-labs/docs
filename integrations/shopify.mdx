Connect Shopify to use your store's products in Moshi sales flows.

## Product and inventory sync

Moshi starts importing your active Shopify products when you connect your store.
After that, Moshi schedules product and inventory syncs every six hours.
New products and inventory changes wait for the next sync, plus processing time.
Shopify product notifications do not trigger an immediate catalog update.

After a sync completes, reload the flow builder to load the updated catalog.
Reloading the builder does not start a Shopify sync.

## Product availability

A product must be visible to Moshi before you can select it for a flow.
If the product has variants, at least one variant must also be visible and available.
Draft, archived, deleted, and disconnected-store products are unavailable through the active Shopify catalog.

Zero inventory does not always prevent a sale.
Moshi treats a variant as sellable when it has positive inventory or Shopify allows continued sales when out of stock.

## A synced product is missing from the flow picker

The flow builder currently loads only the first 100 products, ordered by sales count.
Search and `Show more` use that loaded list.
They do not load the rest of your catalog.

If your store has more than 100 products, a new product can sync successfully and still be absent from the flow picker.
Waiting for another sync or reloading the builder does not remove this limit.
Contact the Moshi team with the product name and Shopify product URL if this limit blocks your flow.
